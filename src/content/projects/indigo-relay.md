## Purpose

Indigo Relay is a private tool gateway with bounded retrieval from authorized sources.

## Selected evidence

A documented November 2026 matched retrieval experiment reported median wall-time reductions of 47.6%, 62.6% and 48.9% in three distinct workflows, with expected recall. Those are historical measurements, not current benchmarks, token savings or cost savings.
